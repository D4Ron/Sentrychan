Installed — open it when you're ready

Sentrychan is in your Applications folder. It doesn't start by itself.

  1. Open it from Applications or Launchpad.
  2. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. That happens once.
  3. On its first start it asks whether to open when you log in — your choice, and you can change it in Settings.

Sentrychan isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, which is why macOS asks. It connects to nothing until you add your own sources.
