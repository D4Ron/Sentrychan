About online sources

Worth understanding before you install, because it shapes what you see on first run.

Sentrychan ships with no online sources built in

Out of the box it is a library manager, downloader and reader. It does not include, host or bundle any scraper for a manga or anime site, and it is not affiliated with any of them.

Online sources come from source packs — separate, third-party plug-in files you can import yourself from Settings → Library. They load on the next restart. Sentrychan neither distributes nor links to them.

Everything else works without any of that: your existing files, your own folders of chapter images, RSS feeds you add, MyAnimeList import, the reader and the quiz.

What Sentrychan expects of you

Sentrychan is a general-purpose tool. It connects to nothing on its own: every feed it reads and every source it can reach is one you configured. It is built for organising a library you are entitled to hold — your own rips, public domain and Creative Commons works, freely distributed releases, and anything you have permission to download.

It is not intended for copyright infringement, and using it that way is neither supported nor endorsed. What you point it at, and whether that is lawful where you live, is your responsibility.

How to use it

The same quick tour that greets you inside the app — here whenever you need it.

1

Add what you're watching

Open the Library, click Add Series, search for a show and pick it. Sentrychan grabs the poster and starts tracking it. Already on MyAnimeList? Import your whole watching list from Settings.

2

Point it at your folders

In Settings → General, set a download folder and a library folder. Finished downloads move from one to the other, renamed and filed into Show / Season folders on the way.

3

Let it handle new episodes

Matches from your feeds are detected and fetched automatically. Turn on auto-download to skip even the confirmation, or review and confirm each one yourself.

4

Fill in the gaps

Fill an entire season in one pass, let Sentrychan spot which episodes are missing from a series you already own and fetch only those, or use the Unmatched Files screen to place anything the matcher couldn't identify.

5

Track & read manga and novels

Switch to Manga or Novels, add a series and read chapters in-app — paged or webtoon — or download them for offline. Point it at a local folder to read what you already have.

6

Manage downloads

The Downloads page gives you pause, resume, speed limits and a queue. Right-click any download to open its folder or copy the link.

7

Tune it & relax

Pick a theme and layout, set your preferred release groups, and let the background monitor and notifications keep everything current.

Installing

One download, no account, no .NET to install — everything's bundled, and it updates itself from then on.

Windows may warn you the first time

Sentrychan is a small free app from an independent developer, so it isn't code-signed yet. Windows SmartScreen may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen on the first launch.

That's expected. Click More info → Run anyway. The warning fades as more people install it.

Manga and novels are still in beta

The anime side is the mature half of Sentrychan. Manga and novel support — the library, the reader, offline downloads and progress tracking — works, but it is newer and rougher, and odd chapter numbering or an unusual source layout can still trip it up.

Feedback on it is genuinely wanted. Tell me what broke, on GitHub issues or Discord, and include the series and source if you can — that's usually enough to reproduce it.

Do I need an account?

No — and today there is nothing to sign up for. Everything Sentrychan currently does runs entirely on your own machine and never talks to a server of ours: library, downloads, monitoring, the reader, the quiz.

The account-backed features are the ones listed under On the way, and they aren't in the app yet. When they arrive they'll be opt-in, and you'll be able to run them against your own Supabase project instead if you'd rather.

Where the money actually goes

Being straight about this: it is not all infrastructure. Part of every contribution goes to me, the developer, as payment for my time. Sentrychan is built by one person in evenings and weekends, and a project that pays for some of those evenings is a project that is still here in two years. I would rather say that plainly than dress it up as server costs.

The rest is the real bills: a code-signing certificate, hosted rooms for watch parties, and the database behind sync and alerts.

The app itself will never be for sale. Not a trial, not a licence, not a paywalled build. Supporting Sentrychan will never get you more sources, faster downloads, or any advantage in finding or fetching content — none of that is a product here and none of it ever will be. The downloader, the library, the reader and the quiz are finished, free, and stay that way for everyone, forever.

And if you would rather not fund the hosting at all, run it yourself: point the app at your own Supabase project, apply schema.sql from the repository, and the same features work against your own backend for nothing. That escape hatch is deliberate and will not be closed.
